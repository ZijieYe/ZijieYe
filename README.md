# Hi, I'm Zijie Ye

I'm a bioinformatician working at the intersection of sequencing technology, computational biology, and software development. My recent work focuses on long-read RNA sequencing, nanopore signal processing, and practical analysis pipelines.

## Research & Engineering

- **Nanopore signal processing:** exploring raw RNA signal preprocessing, read segmentation, gating and noise detection, and adapter identification.
- **Long-read transcriptomics:** developing workflows to compare direct RNA and cDNA sequencing data, with an interest in transcript-level analysis and interpretation.
- **Poly(A) analysis:** benchmarking poly(A) callers and investigating signal-based length estimation and uncertainty.
- **Spatial and targeted sequencing:** working on probe design, barcode processing, spatial sequencing workflows, and targeted sequencing analysis.
- **Machine learning for sequencing:** exploring representations of nanopore RNA signals and their potential for studying RNA modifications.

## Background

I hold Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Bioinformatics from Saarland University, Germany. As a research assistant on the TFmiR3 project, I worked on backend data management, performance optimization, and testing.

My work spans Python, R, C++, Java, shell scripting, and workflow development with WDL. I enjoy building tools that connect biological questions with reliable, interpretable analysis.
